Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our developer network

How are developers verified on the platform?

Each developer undergoes a rigorous vetting process including legal, financial, and project-history review before being listed on our platform.

Can I contact developers directly?

Yes, once you view a developer's profile, you can submit an inquiry form or access their direct contact information to schedule consultations.

What do the developer ratings represent?

Ratings are compiled from client reviews, completion rates, structural audit reports, and general transaction feedback over the past 5 years.

How do I list my own development project?

If you are a licensed developer, you can apply through our Partner Program. Our team will perform a credential check and set up your dashboard.

Is there a fee to browse developers?

No, browsing the developer directory and reading profiles is completely free for all buyers and investors.
