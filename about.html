Frequently Asked Questions

How do I arrange a physical viewing for a listing?

To coordinate viewings, fill in the "Contact Agent" form directly on the property details page or reach out to support@StacklyHouses.com with the listing ID. Our local agent will contact you within 24 hours.

Are there hidden fee commissions when listing my house?

No. We operate under a flat listing commission structure representing 2.5% of final transaction value, with no upfront registration fees or marketing charges.

Does Stackly Houses assist with bank loan approvals?

Yes! We have partnering agreements with major credit unions and banks to assist our buyers in securing competitive mortgage rates and quick pre-approval letters.
